# Bank help knowledge base

## Accounts
- Account types: SAVINGS and CURRENT.
- Account numbers are 10 digits.
- Registering (Register page) creates a customer profile and opens one new account immediately. The opening deposit is optional and can be 0.
- Each customer logs in with their e-mail address and password. Passwords must be 8-64 characters.
- A login session ends after 30 minutes without activity.
- Currency: Indian rupees (INR).

## Beneficiaries
- You can only send money to a saved beneficiary.
- To add one: log in, open **Beneficiaries**, enter the person's 10-digit account number and an optional nickname. The app shows the account holder's name before you save.
- Beneficiaries must hold an account at this bank. Transfers to other banks are not supported.
- You cannot add your own account as a beneficiary, or add the same account twice.
- You can remove a beneficiary at any time from the Beneficiaries page.

## Sending money
- Open **Send Money**, choose the account to send from, pick a beneficiary, enter the amount and an optional note, then review and confirm.
- Transfers are instant. Each one gets a reference number starting with "TXN-".
- Limits: more than 0, at most 2 decimal places, at most 1,000,000 per transfer, and never more than the available balance.
- Transfers cannot be cancelled or reversed once confirmed. Contact the bank if you sent money by mistake.
- The Banking Assistant can prepare a transfer for you from a chat, but nothing moves until you press Confirm.

## Security
- The bank will never ask for your password in chat, e-mail or on the phone.
- Larger or unusual transfers may be reviewed by the bank's fraud team after they happen. If the bank contacts you about one, it is about a transfer you made, and they will not ask for your password.
- If you think someone else has used your account, contact the bank straight away.

## Things the app cannot do yet
- Reset a forgotten password, change your name, e-mail or phone, close an account, or open a second account. Contact the bank for these.
- Statements and downloads are not available yet; the Dashboard shows your recent activity.
